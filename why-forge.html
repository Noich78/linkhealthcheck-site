Why we built Link Healthcheck on Atlassian Forge

A health check reads the structure of your Jira projects. Before installing an app like that, a Jira admin should know where the data goes. Our answer is short: it does not leave Atlassian.

No servers of our own

Link Healthcheck runs entirely on Atlassian Forge, Atlassian's own app platform. There is no GC Consulting server, no external database and no account to create. The app is hosted, authenticated and encrypted by Atlassian.

What the app reads, and what it keeps

  • Read during a scan: for the project you open, each issue's key, summary, type, status, created and updated dates, parent and issue links. It is processed in memory and not stored.
  • Kept in Forge storage: your settings, the findings you chose to ignore, and a 30-day score history.
  • Never read: assignee, reporter, names or e-mail addresses.

Three permissions, nothing else

read:jira-work to scan issues and links, write:jira-work only for the fixes you confirm, and storage:app for settings and history.

Every change is yours

The app changes nothing on its own. A fix runs only after you click and confirm it, with your own Jira account, so it appears in the issue history like any change you make.

What Forge means for you

  • Your security review is shorter: no third party and no sub-processor other than Atlassian.
  • Data stays where your Jira data already is, in your site's Forge storage.
  • When you uninstall the app, Atlassian deletes its data under Forge rules.
