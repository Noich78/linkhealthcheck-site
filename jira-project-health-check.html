How healthy is your Jira project?

A Jira project rarely breaks all at once. It drifts: a blocker link stays after the work is done, two issues end up waiting on each other, an epic stays open with nothing left inside, a bug is never linked to the work it affects. Each one is small. Together they make boards, plans and reports say something that is no longer true.

A project health check finds that drift before it shows up in a missed date.

What a Jira project health check looks at

  • Circular dependencies: issues that block each other in a loop, so none of them can really start.
  • Stale blockers: issues blocked by work nobody has touched for a long time, and "is blocked by" links that point to work already Done.
  • Empty or finished epics: epics with no child issues, or epics still open when every child is Done.
  • Broken hierarchy: open issues under a parent that is already closed.
  • Untraced work: bugs not linked to anything, and work items outside any epic.

One score, and what to fix first

Link Healthcheck gives each Jira project a health score from 0 to 100, with problems grouped into dependencies, epics and traceability. "Fix first" lists the findings that raise the score the most.

On our 390-issue demo project: score 69, At risk, 75 findings, 232 open issues analysed.

Fix it in one click, and see every change first

Remove an obsolete blocker link or close a finished epic, one at a time or up to 20 at once. You see each change before it is made, and it is made with your own Jira account.

What it does not do: it never deletes issues. A fix only removes the link you confirmed, or moves the epic you confirmed to Done.

Questions

Is this the same as Jira's own admin health check?
No. Jira's admin tools look at the configuration of your site. A project health check looks at the work items themselves: their links, epics and traceability.
Does it change my data?
Only when you click a fix, and only the link or epic you confirmed.
How many issues does a scan read?
Up to the 2,000 most recently updated issues of the project.
