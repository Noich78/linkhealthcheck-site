Find

The problems a board never shows you

Your board can look clean while the links underneath are broken. Link Healthcheck reads your project (up to its 2,000 most recently updated issues per scan), runs 8 checks and lists what is wrong, sorted by severity.

Circular dependencies

Issues that block each other in a loop, so none of them can start.

Stale blockers

Issues blocked by work nobody has touched for a long time, or still "blocked by" issues already Done.

Empty or finished epics

Epics with no child issues, or epics still open when every child is Done.

Orphan issues

Bugs linked to nothing, and open issues under a parent that is already closed.

One report

Every finding, with its severity and impact

Each line names the issue, explains the problem in plain words and shows how many points fixing it adds to your score. Open it in Jira or ignore it.

Fix

Clean up your Jira backlog in one click

Two kinds of problems can be fixed in one click: obsolete "blocks" links to issues already Done, and epics whose child issues are all Done. Fix them one by one or up to 20 at once. Every change is listed for you to confirm, and it is made with your own Jira account. For everything else, open the issue in Jira and fix it your way.

Trust

Built on Atlassian Forge, for Jira Cloud

Your data stays in Atlassian

The app runs on Atlassian's own platform. It only keeps your settings, ignored findings and score history. Read the privacy policy.

Nothing changes without you

The scan only reads. A fix happens only when you click it, after you have seen exactly what will change. Settings and "Ignore" are reserved for project administrators.

Permissions requested

  • read:jira-work to scan issues and links
  • write:jira-work only for the fixes you confirm
  • storage:app for settings, ignored findings and score history

FAQ

Questions

How much does it cost?
It is free for teams up to 10 users. Larger teams pay per user through the Atlassian Marketplace, with a free trial.
Which Jira does it work with?
Jira Cloud only. It is not available for Jira Data Center or Server.
How is the score calculated?
The score starts at 100 and goes down with each open finding, weighted by its severity and the size of the project. Each finding shows how many points fixing it adds back.
Which problems can be fixed in one click?
Obsolete "blocks" links to issues that are already Done, and epics whose child issues are all Done. Every other finding opens the issue in Jira so you can decide.
How large a project can it scan?
Each scan reads up to the 2,000 most recently updated issues of the project.
Will it change my issues?
Only when you click a fix. Removing a link leaves both issues unchanged. Closing an epic moves it to Done, and only when all its child issues are already Done.
Who builds it?
GC Consulting, an Atlassian Marketplace partner based in Munich, Germany.
