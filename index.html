What a board does not show you

A board can look clean while the links underneath are broken. Each scan reads up to the 2,000 most recently updated issues of the project and sorts what it finds by severity. What a Jira project health check looks at.

CheckWhat it meansOne-click fix
Circular dependenciesIssues that block each other in a loop, so none of them can start.No
Stale blockersIssues blocked by work nobody has touched for a long time, or still "blocked by" issues already Done.Yes, for Done
Empty or finished epicsEpics with no child issues, or epics still open when every child is Done.Yes, for finished
Orphan issuesBugs linked to nothing, and open issues under a parent that is already closed.No

Every finding explained, every fix confirmed

Each line names the issue, explains the problem in plain words and shows how many points fixing it adds to your score. Open it in Jira or ignore it.

Two kinds of problems can be fixed in one click: obsolete "blocks" links to issues already Done, and epics whose child issues are all Done. Fix them one by one or up to 20 at once.

Every change is listed before it is made, and it is made with your own Jira account.

Questions

How much does it cost?
It is free for teams up to 10 users. Larger teams pay per user through the Atlassian Marketplace, with a free trial.
Which Jira does it work with?
Jira Cloud only. It is not available for Jira Data Center or Server.
How is the score calculated?
The score starts at 100 and goes down with each open finding, weighted by its severity and the size of the project. Each finding shows how many points fixing it adds back.
Will it change my issues?
Only when you click a fix. Removing a link leaves both issues unchanged. Closing an epic moves it to Done, and only when all its child issues are already Done.
Who builds it?
GC Consulting, an Atlassian Marketplace partner based in Munich, Germany.
